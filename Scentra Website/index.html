Material Honesty

Four materials, no substitutes

i.

White Floral

Pale, light-colored blossoms that create clean, romantic, or intensely narcotic profiles in perfumery.

ii.

Vanilla

Warm, and cozy fragrance note used mostly as a rich base note to give perfumes sweetness, depth, and staying power.

iii.

Woody

Built around fragrant essences from trees, bushes, and roots like sandalwood, cedarwood, vetiver, patchouli, and oud

iv.

Citrus

Belong to the Hesperidic family, derived from fruits and citrus-smelling plants that provide a bright, crisp, and refreshing opening to a scent.
